Check a product

Enter the barcode number (UPC), the lot or product code, or both. We search the text of FDA recall notices from FDA.gov, including their code tables, plus FDA's enforcement records. The result shows exactly which dates were searched.

Try: · (recalled) · (no recall)

We check FDA recall notices on FDA.gov (collected every few hours, building up to the last 12 months) and openFDA enforcement records (updated weekly). A code match is a strong sign, not proof; the FDA notice is the authority. This test does not yet check USDA FSIS recalls for meat, poultry and eggs, or FDA's same-day announcements. Always confirm at FDA.gov and FSIS.usda.gov.
